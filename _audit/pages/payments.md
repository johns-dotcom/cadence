## 2. Summary

NEW rebuilt a 4,200-line command center as a 491-line list. The split-family cascade, rush/hold mutex, pay-with-proof (AI date/ref extraction), family-aware selection totals, and Split badge survived; almost all of OLD's operational depth did not. The single worst finding is not a missing feature but a dead one: **NEW's `EmailPreviewModal` is invoked without its required `open` prop, so it renders `null` — Send-for-Approval and every payment-confirmation send on this page opens nothing** (DEF-PAY-01). Beyond that: confirmations for split invoices would state the parent's slice instead of the family total (DEF-PAY-02); the stat cards dropped OLD's never-netted per-currency captions (DEF-PAY-03); the paid-lingering window shrank 14→7 days (DEF-PAY-04); and the Multi-invoice worklist, Batch Payment modal, installments UI, calendar view, search/amount/method/status/rep/group/sort toolbar, per-vendor confirmation bundling, bank-evidence dots, inline row editing + undo, Excel export, and the persistent bottom totals bar are all missing. Totals: **30 defects (10 P1 · 13 P2 · 7 P3), 9 intentional divergences.**

## 4. Interaction & behavior parity

- **Page scope / quick-filter semantics** — OLD: one fetch (`/bk/payments`, unpaid + paid-in-last-14-days server-side), every chip narrows client-side; held rows leave Overdue+Due Soon but stay in Total Unpaid; Rush keeps paid rows visible for 10s via `rushGraceIds` (`BkPayments.jsx:243-268,1276-1301`). Held rows correctly leave Due Soon/Overdue (`:71`), but NEW's **Unpaid chip also excludes held rows** (`:68`) where OLD's Unpaid = everything not Paid (`:1278`) — chip count disagrees with the "Total unpaid" card, DEF-PAY-26. No rush grace (DEF-PAY-25).

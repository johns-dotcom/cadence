## 2. Summary

NEW's 2026-08-27 build is a real reconciliation surface — upload → ingest (dedupe/internal-dismiss/rules/auto-match) → six-disposition mini-ledger with coverage KPI, category strip, bulk bar, review deck, money-in income booking, balances, rules manager, and a global txn search with strong field parity. The one-debit-per-invoice capacity model, prepayment guard, and rejection memory even carry OLD's hardest-won matching invariants. But the **library half of the page is gutted** (flat list; no month grouping, no coverage badges, no overlap warnings, no batch upload, no view-original, no re-parse), the **verification machinery is gone entirely** (deterministic balance-verified parser, extras audit, misfiled repair — the three features whose whole point is proving the app against the statement's own arithmetic), and the **parser regressed on currency**: NEW's PDF prompt and CSV parser capture no currency at all, recreating the exact ¥-as-$ overstatement OLD documents repairing at four separate sites. Ingest also collapses legitimate identical same-day charges (OLD's motivating case was 30 real $1.00 fees in one day), and once a statement is ingested the matcher never runs again — no freshness re-run, no nightly sweep, no rematch-all, no reset. Totals: **24 defects (10 P1 · 9 P2 · 5 P3), 7 intentional divergences.**

## 6. Intentional divergences (not defects)

1. **INT-1 Multi-tenancy**: every table/query label-scoped; router-level `authMiddleware, withTenant, requireAdmin` (`bank-statements.js:19`) replacing OLD's per-route `isStrictAdmin`.
2. **INT-2 Per-label accounts**: `labels.bank_accounts` JSONB + `GET/PUT /accounts` with per-account payment-method hints (`:184-199`) replacing OLD's hardcoded BofA/PayPal `ACCOUNTS` — required for multi-tenant; account-generic parsing follows.
3. **INT-3 IA inversion**: NEW hosts the mini-ledger, review deck, Money-in section, and rules manager on `/bank-statements/:id`; OLD moved that surface to Bank Matching and left the statements page a pure library. `MatchModal` is exported so both NEW surfaces share one dialog (`BankStatements.jsx:448-450`).
4. **INT-4 Manual balance editor**: `Set/Edit balance` prompt + `PATCH /:id/balance` + on-demand `reparse-balance` (`:229-246`; server `:375-434`) — additive UI OLD lacked (OLD's backfill was automatic; its loss is DEF-BST-24).
5. **INT-5 Activity-bot events** on month reconcile/reopen (`:888,896`) — additive, matches cadence's activity-stream convention.
6. **INT-6 View-time suggestions** (`statementSuggest`, never stored, retroactive by design, `:322-331`) replacing OLD's learned-category map for the inline "★ suggested" — different mechanism, defensible.
7. **INT-7 Search deep-link target**: hits open the statement detail with `?q=&chip=` (`:128-131`) instead of forwarding to Bank Matching — consistent with INT-3.
